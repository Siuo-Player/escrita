# Season 1 Recurring Schedules — School, Work, Training and Weekly Habits

Status: WORKING / continuity reference
Updated: 2026-10-08

## How to use this document

These schedules are the default state of the world.

They are not scene lists. A character can be doing something else when an episode needs it, but deviations
should have a reason.

Exact times are mostly **CREATED/WORKING** for this fanfic. They are chosen to be plausible for Southern
California in 2009. When a detail is directly supported by *Modern Family* canon, it is marked as CANON.

The purpose is to make encounters happen because people actually share geography and time, rather than because
the outline needs them to meet.

## Recurring school/learning habits

### Alex

CANON:
- student;
- academically exceptional;
- plays the **cello**, not piano. The series later explicitly references her cello lessons and practice, and
  character material consistently identifies cello as her instrument. citeturn139766search4turn139766search5turn573317search62

WORKING recurring routine:
- cello lesson once per week, usually **late afternoon**;
- 20–40 minutes of cello practice on several other days;
- regular homework/study block most school nights;
- occasional school academic activity/club when relevant;
- Wednesday evening is a useful flexible slot because it can coexist with family/canon activity.

Do not write piano lessons for Alex unless the story explicitly establishes a later AU change.

### Nathan

WORKING:
- programming: Monday, Wednesday and Friday evenings;
- longer programming block Saturday or Sunday;
- football training Tuesday/Thursday;
- Saturday match/activity;
- two short conditioning/running sessions per week;
- martial-arts training every 1–2 weeks, normally Saturday afternoon;
- schoolwork completed before serious late-night programming whenever possible.

### Haley

WORKING:

## Prop and purchase cadence

Do not manufacture a purchase for every object.

Record a provenance only for objects that are likely to recur.

Examples:
- school supplies after the move;
- household décor bought gradually rather than in one giant "new house" scene;
- toys arriving as gifts or purchases;
- electronics being unpacked after the move;
- sports gear associated with football;
- recognizable Nerf equipment appearing after a plausible purchase/gift before the later episode needs it.

## Encounter logic

When planning a scene, check three things:

1. Where is each character supposed to be at that time?
2. What routine would naturally put them there?
3. If they meet, what is the mundane reason?

Only then decide whether the meeting is worth showing.
