# Opening Calendar Audit — September 2009

Status: SOURCE-BACKED CONTINUITY CHECK
Updated: 2026-10-12

## Purpose

The opening timeline uses precise dates for the move, school transfer, birthday, first exchanges with Alex, and *Modern Family* canon anchors. This audit separates externally verifiable dates from the story's working assumptions so a scene outline does not quietly overrule the calendar.

## Verified calendar facts

- **Monday, 7 September 2009** was Labor Day in the United States. The Los Angeles Unified School District's adopted 2009–10 calendar marks 7 September as Labor Day, 8 September as a pupil-free day, and **Wednesday, 9 September as the first day of instruction**. Source: [LAUSD 2009–10 single-track calendar (PDF)](https://www.lausd.net/pdf/Single09-10.pdf).
- The archived Seattle Public Schools 2009–10 calendar also lists **Wednesday, 9 September 2009 as the first day of school**. Sources: [Seattle Public Schools 2009–10 important dates, archived by NCTQ](https://teacherquality.nctq.org/dmsView/61-09_7435) and [Seattle Schools Community Forum, 17 June 2009](https://saveseattleschools.blogspot.com/2009/06/2009-2010-school-calendar-available.html).
- *Modern Family* Season 1 broadcast dates used as the opening canon anchors are: **Pilot — 23 September 2009; The Bicycle Thief — 30 September; Come Fly with Me — 7 October; The Incident — 14 October**. Sources: [Season 1 episode guide](https://modern-family.tv/episodeguide/season/1/) and [TheTVDB official aired order](https://thetvdb.com/series/modern-family/seasons/official/1).

## Working story decisions

- Nathan's birthday remains **7 September 1996**, and the story begins on his thirteenth birthday, **Monday, 7 September 2009**. The date falling on Labor Day is intentional; it gives the family a day together and means this is not a school-day scene.
- For this opening draft, **Seattle Public Schools is the school-calendar baseline** while the family is still in the Seattle area. The particular old school remains unnamed. If the setting later moves to a neighboring district or a specific private school, check that school's 2009 calendar before changing the story dates.
- The old-school term begins on **Wednesday, 9 September**, rather than Tuesday, 8 September. Nathan's last week there is compressed; departure from the Seattle area begins on **Monday, 14 September**, and 15 September remains the California arrival/setup day.
- Nathan's first day at the new Southern California school remains **Wednesday, 16 September**. His practical first interaction with Alex remains **Friday, 18 September**.
- **21 September is an ordinary Monday**, not Nathan's birthday. Any Alex interaction that day must grow from their existing exchange rather than use a mistaken birthday acknowledgment as a shortcut to closeness.

## Why this matters for prose

Calendar dates are continuity anchors, not mandatory scene headings. The birthday can be shown on 7 September; 8 September can be compressed or used for routine family/game preparation; school scenes begin on 9 September. Do not shift the birthday to 21 September to preserve an older outline line. When factual evidence and an obsolete outline disagree, update the outline and preserve the established character decision.
